Podcast Proposal - The Den: Inside Famous Foxes Overview This podcast episode explores the tools and infrastructure within the Famous Fox ecosystem, unpacking how they work and why they were created. Through an in-depth interview with the founder, the episode will give listeners a behind-the-scenes look at the ecosystem’s evolution, design philosophy, and future direction. Tone is conversational but insightful—balancing technical depth with being accessible to the new-to-crypto users. Key Goals - Break down the key tools in the Famous Fox ecosystem in a way that’s easy to understand Main focus: FoxySwap Snapshot + FoxyShare Revoker Fortuna FFFlip - Highlight the founder’s vision, challenges, and decision-making process - Provide value to both newcomers and experienced users - Explore how the ecosystem fits into the broader digital/tech landscape Episode Outline 1. Intro (2–3 min) - Hook: What is the Famous Fox Federation - Who are we, why do we matter - Quick overview of the episode and guest 2. Founder Story (8–10 min) - Background of the founder (Dev and Draxxts) - Origin story of Famous Fox, Mint Day stories - Early challenges and breakthroughs 3. Deep Dive: Ecosystem Tools (15–20 min) - Overview of core tools within the ecosystem - What problems each tool solves - Weekly missions - How users interact with them, constant touch points even when NFTs have been less active - Unique stories or remarkable moments 4. Challenges & Lessons (8–10 min) - Biggest obstacles faced - Mistakes and pivots - In-person events - Key lessons for builders and founders 6. Future Roadmap (5–8 min) - Upcoming tools or features - Long-term vision for the ecosystem - Trends the founder is watching 7. Rapid Fire / Personal Segment (5 min) (can be from Quicks, Gwei can provide if not) - Quick questions (favorites, habits, advice) - Humanizing the founder 8. Closing (2–3 min) - Key takeaways - Where to follow or explore the ecosystem - Outro
