How it works
Simple. Flat rate. Delivered.

Washo removes every friction point from laundry. One bag, one price, one rider. Here's exactly how it works from door to door.

01
🛍️

Buy your Washo bag

Your $12.99 Washo bag is a one-time purchase that lasts a lifetime. It's more than a bag — it's your membership. Each bag ships with a unique QR code tied to your account. No scanning app needed. Just fill it and leave it out.

02
📍

Schedule your pickup

Open the app, pick your time slot, and confirm your address. A Washo rider will arrive during your window, scan your bag, and take it to your nearest partner laundromat. No need to be home — leave it at your door.

03
🌀

We handle everything

Your partner laundromat washes, dries, and folds your clothes to your specs — cold wash, no softener, hang dry, whatever you need. Track the status in real time from pickup to fold.

04
✨

Clean clothes at your door

Your rider delivers your folded clothes back to your door within 24 hours of pickup. Same bag. Same address. No surprises on price — it's always flat rate.
